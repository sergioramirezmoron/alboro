Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoShorts processes information when users connect social media accounts and use content publishing features.

Last updated: August 18, 2026

1. Who we are

AutoShorts is a software tool developed and operated by Alboro.

AutoShorts is designed to help creators prepare and publish short-form video content to supported social media platforms.

For privacy-related questions, you can contact: alboroinfo@gmail.com .

2. Information AutoShorts may process

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, AutoShorts may process the following information:

  • basic account information provided by connected platforms, such as account identifiers, usernames or profile information;
  • OAuth authorization information, including access tokens and, where provided by the platform, refresh tokens;
  • videos and other media files that you choose to publish;
  • titles, captions, descriptions, hashtags, tags and other publishing metadata;
  • information necessary to determine the status of a publishing request;
  • technical information and error logs necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

3. Information we do not request

AutoShorts does not require users to provide their TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or other social media passwords directly to AutoShorts.

Authentication to supported platforms is performed using the authorization mechanisms provided by those platforms, such as OAuth.

4. How information is used

Information processed by AutoShorts may be used to:

  • authenticate authorized social media accounts;
  • prepare and send content selected by the user to supported platforms;
  • create publishing requests and monitor their status;
  • generate or manage titles, descriptions, captions, hashtags and related metadata;
  • maintain the security and reliability of the service;
  • diagnose errors and technical problems;
  • comply with applicable legal, security or platform requirements.

5. TikTok data

If you connect a TikTok account, AutoShorts may receive information made available through TikTok's official APIs and only within the permissions you authorize.

This information may include account identifiers, authorization tokens and information required to upload or publish content.

TikTok information is used only to provide the functionality requested by the user, such as account authorization and content publishing.

6. Content and media

Videos, descriptions, captions and other content are processed when necessary to provide the publishing functionality requested by the user.

AutoShorts does not claim ownership of content uploaded by users.

7. Sharing of information

AutoShorts does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party services when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user.

For example, when a user requests publication to TikTok, the relevant video and publishing information may be transmitted to TikTok through its official API.

Information may also be disclosed if required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary to protect the security of the service.

8. Authentication tokens

Connected platforms may issue access tokens or refresh tokens that allow AutoShorts to perform actions previously authorized by the user.

These credentials are intended to be used only for the operation of authorized features and should be protected against unauthorized access.

9. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested functionality, maintain security, troubleshoot technical issues or comply with applicable obligations.

Temporary media or processing information may be removed after it is no longer necessary for the requested publishing operation.

10. Revoking platform access

Users may revoke AutoShorts' access to a connected social media account through the privacy, security or connected-app settings provided by the relevant platform.

After authorization is revoked, AutoShorts should no longer be able to perform new actions that require that authorization.

11. Data deletion requests

Users may request deletion of personal information associated with AutoShorts by contacting:

alboroinfo@gmail.com

Please include enough information to allow us to identify the relevant AutoShorts account or connected service.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information processed by AutoShorts against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Third-party services

AutoShorts relies on third-party platforms and APIs that operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

This Privacy Policy applies to AutoShorts and does not replace the privacy policies of TikTok, YouTube, Instagram or any other connected service.

14. Children's privacy

AutoShorts is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children below the minimum age required to use the connected services.

15. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AutoShorts changes, new integrations are added or legal and platform requirements change.

The most recent version will always be published on this page.
